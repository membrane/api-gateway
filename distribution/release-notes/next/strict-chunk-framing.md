---
type: breaking
area: HTTP Transport and Runtime
---
- Chunked message bodies are now parsed strictly as RFC 9112 defines them. Every chunk-size line, chunk, trailer line and the end of the body must end with CRLF, and a chunk size must consist of hexadecimal digits only. Before, Membrane accepted any two bytes after a chunk, a bare LF after a chunk extension or in the trailer, a CR followed by any byte at the end of the trailer, a sign or leading whitespace on the chunk size, and a body that ended in the middle of its last line. Such a body is now rejected as unreadable instead of being forwarded: a request is answered with `400` when the gateway reads its body; when the gateway answers without reading the body, its response is sent and the connection is closed. A backend response fails with an error, or with a closed connection if it is already being passed on to the client. Clients and backends that send such framing have to send RFC 9112 conformant chunked bodies; there is no option to accept the old framing. Whitespace after the chunk size and the exact grammar of chunk extensions are still tolerated, as long as the line ends with CRLF.
